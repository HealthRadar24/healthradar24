Privacy notice

HealthRadar24 is operated by Kernelius. This notice describes the current deployment. It must be reviewed before public paid subscriptions are enabled.

The short version

  • The public dashboard can be used without creating an account.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • HealthRadar24 is an intelligence product, not a patient record, clinical system or medical device. Do not submit patient-identifiable or protected health information.
  • You can request access, correction or deletion through HealthRadar24 support.

Information processed

Accounts. Clerk processes identity and session information. Convex stores the account identifier and application records such as preferences and entitlement state.

Usage and reliability. Hosting, CDN, logging and error-monitoring providers may process IP address, user agent, request metadata, diagnostics and security events. Browser preferences may be stored locally.

AI features. Queries and relevant context may be sent to the configured AI provider. Do not enter patient data, credentials, confidential clinical information or other sensitive personal information.

Payments. Public payments are currently disabled. If Stripe billing is activated later, payment-card data will be entered directly into Stripe; HealthRadar24 will store only limited customer, subscription, invoice and entitlement metadata needed to provide the service.

Current service providers

The deployment uses or may use Clerk for authentication, Convex for application data, Vercel for web hosting, Railway for relay and scheduled services, Upstash for Redis-compatible caching, Cloudflare for DNS and edge services, GitHub for source and automation, and configured observability or AI providers when those features are enabled.

Retention and security

Account and entitlement records are retained while an account is active and as needed for security, dispute and legal obligations. Operational logs are kept for bounded provider-configured periods. We use access controls, encrypted transport and least-privilege credentials, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, delete, restrict or object to processing. Use the private route described on the support page. Requests may require identity verification.

Changes

We will update this notice when providers, healthcare features or commercial processing materially change.
